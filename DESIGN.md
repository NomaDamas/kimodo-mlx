# DESIGN.md — Kimodo comparison stage

Reading this as: a lab comparison page for engineers, with a motion-stage language,
leaning toward a dark instrument panel and one amber tracking light.

## Product
A single HTML report that plays the same Kimodo clip from every backend that
actually ran, and a measured timing table. Not a marketing landing page.

## Users
Engineers deciding whether the Apple Silicon port is worth using.

## Signature
Three stick-figure stages on a graphite floor grid. One amber playhead is the
only chromatic accent. Numbers are tabular, not decorative.

## Tokens
- bg: #14120f
- panel: #1e1b16
- ink: #f3ece0
- muted: #9a917f
- amber: #e0a24b
- fail: #c45c4a
- pass: #7ea36b
- type: "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, serif for titles
- mono: "IBM Plex Mono", ui-monospace, monospace for numbers
- radius: 2px
- space: 8 / 16 / 28 / 48

## Motion
Play/pause on the shared playhead. No looping hero animation besides the clip.

## Anti-slop
No purple gradients, no three equal feature cards, no Inter, no glassmorphism.
